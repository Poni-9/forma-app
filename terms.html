2. What the service is

FORMAI is an assistant for planning training and nutrition. From your profile, activities and goals it composes a weekly plan, tracks what you complete, and suggests meals. Part of the content is generated by artificial intelligence.

  • It is not a medical device and is not intended for the diagnosis, treatment or prevention of disease.
  • It is not a substitute for a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or personal trainer.
  • Content generated by AI may be wrong or incomplete. Judge it with your own sense and listen to your body.

3. Age

The service is intended for people aged 15 or over. Younger users (the app accepts ages from 12) use it only with the permission and help of a parent or guardian, who confirms sign-in and AI processing for them. For anyone under 18, FORMAI does not plan weight loss — it plans maintenance and building fitness, because the body is still growing. Weight loss at that age belongs under medical supervision, not in an app.
