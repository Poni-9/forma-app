Important: FORMAI provides coaching and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. Calorie and training-load figures are estimates. Before making significant changes to your training or diet, consult a doctor — especially if you have a health condition.

This is an English translation of the Slovenian privacy policy published at formai.si/zasebnost.html. In case of any discrepancy, the Slovenian version prevails.

What data we process

  • Account (if you sign in): the name, email address and photo of your Google account.
  • Profile: name (if you enter it), age, sex, height, weight, heart rate, FTP, goals and races.
  • Activities: rides and workouts (distance, duration, elevation, heart rate, power, GPS track) — entered manually, imported, or read from screenshots; optionally a perceived-effort rating (“How did it go?”) and a note you add yourself.
  • Nutrition: meal entries, photographs of food and meal descriptions (typed or dictated) that you submit for analysis.
  • Wellbeing: the morning check-in you fill in yourself (sleep, soreness, energy and, optionally, weight, waist and a note) and — only if you turn it on — sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from your watch via intervals.icu or Garmin Connect (with Garmin also stress, Body Battery and body composition — see the “Garmin Connect” section). If you tap “I’m sick”, also the sick period (from–to), so those days become rest days without a calorie deficit; this is never sent to the AI.
  • Place for the weather (optional): a place you type in, or your device location rounded to about 1 km.
  • Your conversation with the coach and AI output (reviews, meal plan), so you can see them later.

Legal basis

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for health-related data — weight, heart rate, sleep and HRV, sick period, health notes — explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time (see “Your rights”); withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it. For anyone under 15, consent (sign-in, AI) is given by a parent or guardian.
  • Performance of the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): storing and synchronising your data, building your training plan and nutrition targets.

Where your data is held

  • Locally by default: all data is stored in your browser (IndexedDB) on your own device. Until you sign in, it stays there; the services under “Other services” receive only what is listed for them.
  • Synchronisation when signed in: when you sign in with a Google account, your data is automatically stored in your account in Google Firebase (Firestore) as well, so it is available on all your devices. There is no separate switch — synchronisation applies for as long as you are signed in. GPS tracks, meal photos, and sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from your watch are not uploaded to the cloud (the exception is the direct Garmin Connect connection, where Garmin sends the data to our server — see the “Garmin Connect” section). For sign-in, Firebase Authentication receives the name, email address and photo of your Google account.
  • AI (coach, reviews, meal analysis): works only when you are signed in and have allowed AI processing. When you use an AI feature, we send: the numbers from your profile (age, sex, height, weight, FTP, heart rate, goals and races), a summary of your training (for strength sessions also exercises, sets and weights), plan and nutrition, your morning check-in (sleep, energy, soreness and your note, if you wrote one), the text of your question or conversation, your »my rules« for the coach (Pro, if you write them), the coach's notes from older conversations (a short summary you can see and delete in Trener → »spomin in pravila«), a summary of your logged meals for the last 7 days (calories and protein) and the weather forecast for the coming days (without place name or coordinates), meal photos and descriptions you submit for analysis, and images you attach for the coach (e.g. a physique you would like to have). Workout images (watch screen, gym board, sheet) and — if you are 18 or older — a photo of yourself for exercise suggestions are sent to the AI (Google Gemini) for reading only and only with your consent; we never store them (not on the device, in the cloud or on the server). We do not send your name, email address, exact location or GPS tracks, activities from Strava, or sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from your watch.
  • For FORMAI Pro users (by subscription or by invitation), building the plan (questions, weekly proposal, negotiation, exercises and strategy) is done by the Anthropic Claude API (Anthropic PBC, USA) as our contractual processor under Anthropic’s data processing addendum. Anthropic does not use API content to train its models and automatically deletes it, as a rule, within 30 days (longer only if its safety systems flag it for a usage-policy violation or if the law requires it). Requests run through the same server of ours; the key is never in the browser. Because thinking takes several minutes, the server temporarily keeps the coach’s answer in Google Firebase (Firestore) until the app collects it — at most about two hours, then it is deleted; your images and the prompt are not stored.
  • All other AI features (and plan building for all other users) are processed by the Google Gemini API as our contractual processor. We use the paid tier, under which Google does not use the content to train its models; it is used only to produce the response returned to you (Google may keep it for a limited time solely to detect abuse). Requests run through our own server (Google Cloud Run, europe-west1); the API key is never exposed in the browser. Our server does not store the content — it records only the number of AI actions per day and the AI usage (tokens) per month (for the Basic and Pro limits) and a short entry per request without content (time, model, success, token counts). Google does not guarantee the region in which it processes the content for this service.
  • Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from your watch (via intervals.icu) stay only on the device where you turned them on: they are not uploaded to the cloud and never sent to AI. The switch applies to each device separately; when you turn it off, this data is deleted from the device immediately. For health data from Garmin Connect see the “Garmin Connect” section — it is never sent to AI either.
  • Transfers outside the EU: Anthropic and Google may process data outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the USA); such transfers are covered by both providers’ data processing terms with the EU standard contractual clauses.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

Other services

Your browser calls these services directly, so each request also reveals your IP address to them.

  • Open-Meteo (weather forecast, only if you set a place): approximate coordinates of the place — the app rounds them to about 1 km, and a request is never more precise than about 100 m — and, if you search for the place by name, the name you typed.
  • Dictation (microphone button): if you are signed in and have allowed AI, the voice recording is sent through our server to Google Gemini solely to transcribe it into text; we do not store the recording (neither on the server nor in the cloud) — only the text you see and submit goes into the field. Without signing in, speech is turned into text by your browser’s or phone’s speech recognition (e.g. Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari); FORMAI then never receives audio.
  • BRouter (brouter.de, generating new routes): the start point (where your rides usually begin, rounded to about 100 m), a few waypoints of the new route (e.g. the foot of a climb you already ride) and the road-bike settings. Your full ride tracks are never sent.
  • OpenStreetMap (map tiles): when you open a map, your browser downloads tiles from OpenStreetMap servers, which thereby see the area shown.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of formai.si) and Google Cloud Run (our server) record the IP address and time of each request in their server logs.
  • Fonts are served from formai.si — your browser does not call Google Fonts or any other external font service.

Connected services (intervals.icu, Garmin, Strava, Wahoo)

If you connect an external service yourself, we import only what the training features need, and only for as long as the connection is enabled.

  • intervals.icu: when you connect (sign in with intervals.icu or a personal API key) we read your completed activities (duration, distance, elevation gain, heart rate, power, zones, device, GPS track), your FTP and zones and — only if you separately turn it on — sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. We write planned workouts from FORMAI to your intervals.icu calendar so they reach your watch. The access token or key is stored in the app data on your device and, while you are signed in, in your account (synchronisation); it is used only to read your data and write workouts. With sign-in via intervals.icu, our server exchanges the authorisation code for the token and does not store the token. You can disconnect at any time in Settings or in your intervals.icu settings.
  • Garmin: data from Garmin devices reaches FORMAI directly from Garmin Connect (see the “Garmin Connect” section below), via intervals.icu or from files you import yourself. We use it exclusively to display your progress, compute training load, and adapt your training plan. Garmin data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or profiling. You can delete imported activities at any time together with the rest of your data.
  • Strava: if you connect Strava in Import (“Poveži s Stravo”), our server reads summaries of your activities from the last 7 days (sport, start, time, distance, elevation gain, heart rate, power, calories) — no GPS tracks or maps. Under Strava’s rules we use them only to show them to you and for the day’s expenditure (calories count towards today’s food target); they are never sent to AI, not even indirectly (they are hidden in feedback screenshots). On your device and, when you are signed in, in your account they are kept for at most 7 days and then deleted automatically. Strava access tokens are held only on our server, never in the browser; “Odklopi Stravo” (Disconnect Strava) makes the server revoke access at Strava and delete the tokens, and deleting your account deletes them together with the account. You can also revoke access in Strava (Settings → My Apps). The same applies to Strava-origin activities that arrive via intervals.icu: you see them, they are not sent to AI and their tracks are not stored.
  • Wahoo: if you connect Wahoo (“Poveži z Wahoo”), our server receives summaries of your workouts (sport, time, distance, elevation gain, power, heart rate, calories) and, if you choose, sends planned workouts and a route to your ELEMNT. The summaries are sent to AI like those from other devices; Wahoo does not send GPS tracks. Wahoo access tokens are held only on our server. “Odklopi Wahoo” (Disconnect Wahoo) makes the server revoke access at Wahoo and remove our workouts from your ELEMNT; imported workouts stay in the app as your activities. The same applies when you delete your account.

Garmin Connect

You can choose to connect FORMAI directly to Garmin Connect (Garmin Connect Developer Program). The connection is voluntary and you turn it on yourself in Settings: before connecting you see this notice and confirm your consent, then we redirect you to Garmin’s sign-in, where you choose what to share with FORMAI (activities, health, workouts, courses, women’s health). Without the connection FORMAI receives nothing from Garmin.

  • What is transferred:
    • Activities (always, while the connection is on): summaries (sport, start, duration, distance, elevation, calories, average and maximum heart rate, cadence, speed, device) and details (power, heart rate, cadence and route). From the details our server computes average and normalised power, maximum heart rate, cadence and a simplified GPS track; the raw second-by-second samples are not stored. Garmin does not send history — only activities recorded after connecting arrive.
    • Health (only if you separately turn it on): sleep (duration, stages, score), HRV, resting heart rate, stress, Body Battery, body composition (weight, body fat, muscle mass) and Garmin’s fitness estimate (VO2max).
    • Menstrual cycle (only if you separately turn it on): cycle day, length and phase. It is used exclusively for display and for gentle adjustments on your device (e.g. an offer to ease a hard day, a nutrition note); it is never sent to AI and never changes your calorie target.
  • Where it is held: Garmin sends the data to our server (Google Cloud Run, europe-west1), which stores it in your record in Google Firebase (Firestore, EU region); the app then downloads it to your device. The Garmin access tokens are kept only on the server, never in the browser or in synchronised data. Health and cycle data are additionally kept on the device where you turned them on; they are not synchronised between devices.
  • What is sent to AI: the same as before — activity summaries and the computed readiness score (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, see “Where your data is held”). We never send raw health data from Garmin (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, stress, Body Battery, body composition) and never cycle data.
  • What is sent to your watch: if you turn on sending, our server writes the planned FORMAI workouts for the next 14 days (name, steps, power or heart-rate targets) to your Garmin Connect and, on your request, a ride’s course (Garmin Training and Courses API). When you change the plan or remove a workout, it is updated or removed there as well.
  • Deletion: when you tap “Odklopi Garmin” (Disconnect Garmin) in Settings, the server immediately revokes the connection with Garmin, deletes the tokens and all Garmin data on the server and removes the workouts we sent to your watch, and the app deletes health and cycle data from the device. Imported activities remain in the app as your workouts — you delete them together with your data. The same happens when you delete your account. If you revoke access in Garmin Connect, Garmin sends us a deregistration and we delete the data immediately; if you change permissions there, Garmin notifies us and we only transfer what is still allowed.
  • Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for health and cycle data explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with the switches or “Odklopi Garmin” in Settings, or in Garmin Connect. Garmin data is never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for advertising or profiling.
  • Garmin: for Garmin Connect, Garmin is an independent controller; its privacy policy applies: garmin.com/privacy/connect. Wherever Garmin data is shown, the source is attributed (“Garmin” and the device model). Garmin, Garmin Connect and the Garmin logo are trademarks of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries. FORMAI is not a Garmin product; it is not developed, endorsed or warranted by Garmin.

Payments (FORMAI Pro)

  • You pay for the FORMAI Pro subscription through Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland). You enter your card (or other payment method) details directly on Stripe’s payment page — FORMAI never sees or stores them.
  • What we keep: the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the subscription status (e.g. active, cancelled, payment overdue), the end of the paid period, the source of Pro (subscription or invitation) and payment records (amount, currency, date, payment identifier), and your email address so that we can link the payment to your account.
  • Stripe is our processor for carrying out the payment; for fraud prevention and its own legal obligations (e.g. anti-money-laundering) it processes data as an independent controller under its privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. Stripe may also process data outside the EU (e.g. in the USA), which is covered by the EU standard contractual clauses.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR); keeping payment records: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR — accounting and tax rules).
  • Retention: the subscription status until you delete your account; payment records for as long as accounting and tax rules require — after account deletion we keep them without your email address.

Usage statistics

  • What: so that we know what works in the app and where users get stuck, we record app opens, the screens you open events (e.g. “onboarding finished”, “plan built”, “workout done”, “meal logged”, “AI error”) and which buttons and tabs you tap (only the button’s name, e.g. “Začni trening” / Start workout, and the screen it is on) with the time of the event, the app version, whether it is installed or opened in a browser, whether it is a phone or a computer, and the domain of the page you came from. No content: we do not record what you typed, asked or ate (nothing from text fields and no messages), no numbers (weight, calories, minutes …), your profile numbers or your workouts.
  • No cookies: for visitors we use no cookies or other identifiers stored on the device. To count distinct visitors, the server computes a daily hashed identifier from the IP address and browser type (with a random value that changes every day); the IP address and browser type are not stored.
  • Signed-in users: if you are signed in, events are linked to your account (first and last visit, number of opens and events, version, device type) and we keep a timeline: when you opened which screen, what you tapped and what you did (e.g. “10:42 Plan → taps Start workout”), so that we can see where things go wrong, which features are used and which are not, and help you. Without signing in nothing is linked to a person — we only count totals.
  • Purpose and legal basis: improvements and bug fixing; legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Only the controller (the FORMAI developer, Blaž Gregorc) sees this data in the admin dashboard, to which nobody else has access; we do not use it for advertising and do not share it with third parties.
  • Retention: daily summaries (counters only, without identity) for 25 months; the daily visitor identifier for 2 days; the tap and screen timeline of your account for 60 days (older days are deleted automatically); total counters linked to your account until you delete your account. Deleting your account also deletes the timeline.
  • Turning it off: in Settings, turn off “Pošiljaj statistiko uporabe” (Send usage statistics) — this applies to all your signed-in devices (the setting is synchronised) and to the landing page on this device.

Short in-app polls

  • After onboarding (never in the first 2 days) the app asks at most one short question every 7 days on the Today screen (e.g. “What are you missing in FORMAI?” or a 0–10 rating). Answering is optional — “Ne zdaj” (Not now) skips it.
  • We send: the question ID, the chosen answer, optionally your short comment (up to 194 characters), a random identifier and the app version; if you are signed in, the answer is linked to your account.
  • Only the controller sees the answers and uses them solely to improve FORMAI; they are kept until you delete your account (answers given without signing in until deletion on request).

Weekly question (test programme)

  • After your first week, the app asks at most one question per week (answer A–D). You can skip the question or turn the questions off (“don’t ask again”).
  • If you answer, we send our server only: the question ID, your answer, a random identifier instead of your name or email, the invite code (if you came via a link with a code), the number of days you have used the app, and the app version. No training, nutrition or wellbeing data is sent.
  • The random identifier is stored in your settings, which are synchronised when you sign in, so the answers are pseudonymous, not anonymous.
  • Only the developer sees the answers, and uses them solely to improve FORMAI.

Feedback (the “Feedback” button)

  • You send feedback yourself with the “Feedback” button: your text (or dictated text — speech recognition is done by your browser), the feedback type and optionally a screenshot, which you see before sending and can remove. The screenshot may contain your data that was on screen (e.g. weight or meals); Strava activities are hidden in it.
  • Technical data is attached: screen, app version, screen size, browser type, language and the last app error messages — and your email if you are signed in (so we can reply), otherwise a random device identifier.
  • Feedback is stored in our Google Firebase (Firestore) and only the developer sees it. To find and fix bugs, the developer uses the AI assistant Anthropic Claude (USA), which reads the feedback and the screenshot.
  • The screenshot is deleted once the feedback is resolved; the text is kept for at most 12 months. Earlier deletion: write to the contact below.

How long we keep data

  • On your device: until you delete it.
  • In the cloud (synchronised data, AI counters and request entries, Pro and subscription status, usage statistics of your account, weekly-question and short-poll answers): until you delete your account; the tap and screen timeline for 60 days.
  • Payment records: for as long as accounting and tax rules require (without your email address after account deletion). Usage statistics: daily summaries for 25 months, the daily visitor identifier for 2 days.
  • Server logs (Google Cloud Run, GitHub Pages): for as long as Google’s or GitHub’s default rules provide.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Export: Settings → “Izvozi kopijo” (Export a copy) downloads all of your data at any time (a JSON file). The intervals.icu key is not included — it stays on your device and in your account.
  • Deletion: Settings → “Izbriši račun” (Delete account) deletes your account and all cloud data — synchronised data, AI counters and request entries, Pro status, usage statistics of your account, weekly-question answers, the Strava and Wahoo tokens and the Garmin tokens and data (the connection with Garmin is revoked); an active Pro subscription is cancelled, and payment records are kept without your email address (accounting); the deletion is carried out by our server. “Izbriši na napravi” (Delete on this device) clears the data on the device. You can also write to us — we delete the data within 30 days.
  • Access and correction: all of your data is visible and editable directly in the app.
  • Withdrawing consent: turn off AI processing in Settings (“AI obdelava”), and sleep, HRV and resting heart rate from your watch with the switch next to the intervals.icu connection or with the switches and “Odklopi Garmin” next to the Garmin Connect connection; you can also delete your data or account.
  • You also have the right to restriction of processing and to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (ip-rs.si).
